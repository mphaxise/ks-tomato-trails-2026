# Success Metrics: Fog Belt Scoring System

## The Fog Belt Score (FBS)

At the end of the season, each variety receives a composite **Fog Belt Score (FBS)** out of 100. This is the single number that answers: "Would I grow this again in a Sausalito garden?"

---

## Scoring Dimensions

### 1. Total Yield (25 points)

Cumulative harvest weight across the full season.

| Points | Yield |
|---|---|
| 25 | 1,500g+ |
| 20 | 1,000–1,499g |
| 15 | 500–999g |
| 10 | 200–499g |
| 5 | 50–199g |
| 0 | Under 50g (or no ripe fruit) |

*For cherry varieties: adjust thresholds down by 30% since per-fruit weight is lower but flavor/productivity often compensates.*

---

### 2. Ripening Speed (25 points)

Days from transplant to first ripe fruit harvested.

| Points | Days to First Harvest |
|---|---|
| 25 | Under 60 days |
| 20 | 60–69 days |
| 15 | 70–79 days |
| 10 | 80–89 days |
| 5 | 90–99 days |
| 0 | 100+ days or never ripened |

Speed matters disproportionately in Sausalito's climate — every day earlier means more production before October rains arrive.

---

### 3. Disease Resistance (20 points)

Based on peak fungal pressure score across the season. Lower is better.

| Points | Peak Fungal Score (season high) |
|---|---|
| 20 | 1 (no visible disease all season) |
| 16 | 2 (minor lower-leaf spots only) |
| 12 | 3 (moderate, stayed controlled) |
| 6 | 4 (progressed into upper canopy) |
| 0 | 5 (severe decline from disease) |

---

### 4. Flavor (15 points)

Average of all flavor scores recorded during harvest weeks.

| Points | Average Flavor Score |
|---|---|
| 15 | 9–10 |
| 12 | 7–8 |
| 8 | 5–6 |
| 4 | 3–4 |
| 0 | 1–2 or no data |

---

### 5. Plant Vigor / Grower Experience (15 points)

Subjective end-of-season assessment. How was it to grow?

| Points | Assessment |
|---|---|
| 15 | Robust, easy to manage, thrived with minimal intervention |
| 12 | Healthy overall, manageable |
| 8 | Some struggle but still productive |
| 4 | Constant problems, required heavy management |
| 0 | Failure — would not attempt again |

## Tier Definitions

| Tier | FBS Range | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Fog Champion** | 80–100 | Grow every year. Recommend to every Sausalito/Marin gardener. |
| **Strong Performer** | 65–79 | Reliable producer. Worth including most seasons. |
| **Marginal** | 45–64 | Works with extra care. Not the first choice for fog gardens. |
| **Fog Failure** | Under 45 | Not recommended for Sausalito conditions. |

---

## Minimum Viable Win

For this trial to be a success, we want at least:
- **3 varieties** scoring 65+ FBS points
- **1 variety** scoring 80+ (a true Fog Champion)
- A clear data story explaining *why* winners won (DTM? Disease resistance? Cold-set genetics?)

That gives your friend a concrete, evidence-based planting list for 2027 — and a guide she can share with other Marin fog-belt growers.
