# V1.15 Phase 1 Mid-Run Identification Retrospective

Date: `2026-03-14`

## Scope

This retrospective covers the attempted identification of the middle Phase 1 intake runs between the locked anchors:

- `2026-02-27` = `Day 1 of Phase 1`
- `2026-03-11` = `Last Day of Phase 1`

The target window was the set of mid-run photos between those two dates.

## What We Tried

- Anchor-locked pot/varietal continuity from the trusted first-day and last-day runs.
- Pot-focused CV and label-band OCR to recover pot IDs from the crowded middle-run frames.
- Evidence scoring, ambiguity ranking, and manual review queues for the hardest rows.
- Pairwise cleanup attempts for duplicate-pot conflicts within the same run.

## Where We Reached

- The Phase 1 anchor lock itself remains valid and stays canonical.
- The locked pot-series manifest was corrected so `29T` through `32T` are all varietal `7`, including `31T -> 7`.
- A small number of collision-free manual review fixes were promoted into the canonical row overrides:
  - `2026-03-02` row `153` -> `24T / 12`
  - `2026-03-03` row `201` -> `25T / 5`
  - `2026-03-05` row `335` -> `31T / 7`
- Declared missing/duplicate controls that came from direct review were retained:
  - `2026-03-03` row `221` locked to `30T`, with `25T` declared missing for that intake
  - `2026-03-04` duplicate cleanup retained, with `18T` declared missing for that intake

## Failure Conclusion

This experiment did not produce a reliable middle-run pot-ID identification method.

The core failure mode was crowded multi-pot spill scenes: too many frames contained several visible pots, partial tags, and overlapping foliage for the CV/OCR pipeline to identify one canonical pot reliably enough for production use. OCR and continuity scoring could help rank candidates, but they were not dependable enough to serve as canonical evidence for the middle runs.

## Canonical Decision

Keep only the trusted work in canon:

- the Phase 1 anchor lock
- the corrected pot-series manifest
- the explicitly reviewed collision-free row fixes
- the direct missing-pot / duplicate-row directives

Do not treat the broader mid-run CV/OCR identification outputs, review queues, or experimental history pages as canonical mapping evidence.
