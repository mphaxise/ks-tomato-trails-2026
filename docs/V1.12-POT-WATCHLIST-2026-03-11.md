# V1.12 Pot Watchlist for 2026-03-11 Batch

- The `2026-03-11` photos use a noticeably tighter, brighter, more isolated single-pot framing.
- That makes identity review easier, but it can make plants look smaller than they did on `2026-03-07` even when the plant itself is not necessarily worse.
- Treat `needs_eyeball_check` as "worth an in-person look" rather than "definitely declining."
- Manual two-run reconciliation (completed 2026-03-13) flagged `21T` as missing in the `2026-03-11` capture set.

- `healthy_or_clearer`: plants that look more obviously established or easier to read in the latest batch

- `needs_eyeball_check`: latest photo still reads sparse or weaker even after allowing for the framing shift
